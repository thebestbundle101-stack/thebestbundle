Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how TheBestBundle handles information when visitors use our website, contact us, subscribe to updates, download resources or purchase products.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on how you interact with TheBestBundle, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Information submitted through contact, support or review forms.
  • Information associated with a purchase or customer support request.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when subscribing to updates.

3. Payment Information

Eligible payments for TheBestBundle products and donations may be processed through Stripe.

Payment card information submitted through Stripe Checkout is processed by Stripe. TheBestBundle does not directly store complete payment card numbers through its website checkout pages.

Payment Security

Customers may be directed to Stripe's payment environment to complete eligible transactions. Stripe processes payment information according to its own policies.

4. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by services used to operate or analyze the site.

This may include information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited, referring pages, interaction information and similar usage data.

5. Analytics

TheBestBundle may use analytics services, including Google Analytics, to better understand website traffic and how visitors interact with the website.

Depending on the configuration, analytics services may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information such as browser, device, session and approximate geographic information.

Analytics services operate according to their own privacy policies and available privacy controls.

6. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and improve TheBestBundle.
  • Provide digital products and services.
  • Respond to customer questions and support requests.
  • Process or assist with purchases.
  • Send requested updates or newsletter communications.
  • Understand website usage and performance.
  • Detect misuse, fraud or security issues.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Contact, Support and Review Forms

When you submit information through a contact, support or review form, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, product information and message.

This information may be used to respond to your request, provide customer support and manage communications related to TheBestBundle.

If you voluntarily subscribe to TheBestBundle updates, your email address may be used to send information about new products, resources, bundles, special offers, launches and important TheBestBundle updates.

Where an unsubscribe option is provided, you may use it to stop receiving applicable marketing communications.
