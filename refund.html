Our Refund Policy

Please read the following information before submitting a refund request.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Digital Products

TheBestBundle primarily sells digital products, including eBooks, templates, planners, guides and digital bundles.

Digital products may become accessible shortly after a successful purchase. Because digital files cannot be physically returned in the same manner as traditional merchandise, different refund considerations may apply.

!

Important

Please review the product description, format, included materials and applicable license before completing your purchase.

2. Refund Requests

Refund requests for digital products are reviewed based on the circumstances of the purchase and applicable law.

Submitting a refund request does not automatically guarantee that a refund will be approved.

3. Situations We May Review

Please contact TheBestBundle if you experience an issue such as:

  • Being charged more than once for the same transaction.
  • Receiving the wrong digital product.
  • Being unable to access a purchased product because of a verified delivery problem.
  • Receiving a digital file that is materially defective or unusable.
  • Another purchase issue that requires individual review.

4. Change of Mind

Refunds may not be available solely because a customer changes their mind after purchasing or accessing a digital product, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

5. Product Compatibility

Customers are responsible for reviewing available product information and ensuring they have suitable software or devices for the digital format being purchased.

If you are unsure about a product before purchasing, please contact TheBestBundle for additional information.

6. Resell Rights Products

Certain products may include a Resell Rights (RR) License.

Customers are responsible for reviewing and following the license included with the applicable product.

A misunderstanding of clearly stated license terms does not automatically qualify a purchase for a refund.

7. Bundles

Digital bundles may contain multiple products sold together as one package.

Refund requests involving bundles are reviewed based on the complete transaction rather than individual files within the bundle, subject to applicable law.

8. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you were accidentally charged more than once for the same purchase, contact us with the relevant transaction information so the issue can be reviewed.

9. Payment Processing

Eligible payments are processed through Stripe.

When a refund is approved, processing and the time required for funds to appear may depend on Stripe, the customer's bank, card issuer or payment method.

10. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe a payment method was used without authorization, contact your payment provider or financial institution promptly and notify TheBestBundle so the transaction can be reviewed where appropriate.

11. Future Physical Merchandise

TheBestBundle may introduce physical merchandise through independent third-party vendors or fulfillment partners in the future.

Physical merchandise may be subject to separate return, refund, shipping and cancellation policies provided by the applicable vendor.

12. Applicable Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy is intended to remove or limit consumer rights that cannot legally be waived under applicable law.

13. Changes to This Policy

TheBestBundle may update this Refund Policy as products, services or checkout systems develop.

Any updated version may be posted on this page with a revised update date.

14. Contact Us

If you experience a problem with a purchase, please contact TheBestBundle and provide enough information for the transaction to be reviewed.
