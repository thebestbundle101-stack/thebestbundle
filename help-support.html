Need Assistance?

When contacting support, please include as much relevant information as possible so we can better understand your question or issue.

1. Getting Help

If you have a question about TheBestBundle, the fastest place to start is the FAQ page.

If your question is not answered there, you can contact us through the Contact page.

2. Purchases

If you have a question about a purchase, please provide the relevant information when contacting support.

Depending on the nature of your request, useful information may include the product name, the email address used during the purchase and any relevant order or transaction information available to you.

3. Digital Product Downloads

TheBestBundle products are digital products. After a successful purchase, delivery may take place electronically through the applicable delivery process.

If you experience a problem accessing a purchased digital product, please contact support and explain what happened.

  • Tell us which product you purchased.
  • Tell us the email address associated with the purchase.
  • Describe the problem you are experiencing.
  • Include any relevant information that may help us understand the issue.

4. Checkout & Payment

Eligible purchases are processed through the checkout system provided for the applicable TheBestBundle product.

If a checkout problem occurs, please do not repeatedly submit the same payment if you are unsure whether the transaction was completed. Instead, check your payment information or contact support for assistance.

5. Refund Questions

If you have a question about a refund, cancellation or related purchase issue, please review the Refund Policy and contact us if you need further assistance.

6. Website Problems

If a page, button or website feature does not appear to work correctly, please tell us which page you were using and describe what happened.

If possible, including the name of the product or page and the device or browser you were using may help us understand the issue.

7. Contact Support

For questions that are not answered on this page or in the FAQ, please contact TheBestBundle directly.
