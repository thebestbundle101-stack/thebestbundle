SECURITY • PAYMENTS • CHECKOUT

A Secure Purchasing Experience

TheBestBundle uses Stripe for eligible payment processing and provides customers with a clear checkout process.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Our Checkout Process

TheBestBundle is committed to providing customers with a clear and secure purchasing experience for eligible digital products.

Depending on the purchase method, customers may review their selected products before continuing to the payment processor to complete the transaction.

2. Payment Processing

Eligible TheBestBundle payments are processed through Stripe.

When you continue to Stripe Checkout, payment information is submitted through Stripe's payment environment.

Payment Information

TheBestBundle does not directly store complete credit or debit card numbers entered through Stripe Checkout. Payment information is processed by Stripe according to its applicable security practices and policies.

3. How Checkout Works

1

Choose Your Product

Select the digital product, template, eBook or bundle you would like to purchase.

2

Review Your Selection

When available, review your selected product or cart before continuing to payment.

3

Continue to Secure Payment

Continue to Stripe to enter the payment information required to complete your purchase.

4

Receive Your Product

After successful payment, follow the applicable delivery instructions to access your digital purchase.

Payments Powered by Stripe

Stripe is the independent payment processor used for eligible TheBestBundle transactions.

4. TheBestBundle Cart

When our shopping cart is available, customers may add eligible products, review their selections and remove products before proceeding to checkout.

Adding an item to the cart does not complete a purchase. Payment must still be successfully completed through the applicable checkout process.

5. Buy Now

Certain products may also provide a Buy Now option for customers who want to purchase a single product without continuing to shop.

6. Prices

Customers should review the displayed product price and checkout information before completing payment.

The final amount presented during checkout should be reviewed before payment authorization.

7. Successful Payments

A purchase is considered complete after the applicable payment processor confirms successful payment.

If payment is declined or incomplete, access to the applicable paid product may not be provided.

8. Digital Delivery

Digital products may be delivered through a download page, confirmation page, secure link, email or another method identified for the applicable product.

Customers should retain relevant purchase confirmation information in case support is required.

9. Suspicious Transactions

Transactions may be reviewed, declined or restricted when necessary to address suspected fraud, unauthorized activity, payment disputes or security concerns.

10. Unauthorized Payments

If you believe your payment method was used without authorization, contact your bank or payment provider promptly.

You may also contact TheBestBundle so available transaction information can be reviewed where appropriate.

11. Refunds

Refund requests are subject to our Refund Policy and applicable consumer rights.

12. Privacy

Information associated with website use and purchases is also subject to our Privacy Policy and the applicable policies of independent service providers.

13. Third-Party Checkout Services

When you leave TheBestBundle and interact with an independent payment processor or future merchandise vendor, that provider's own terms, privacy policies and security practices may apply.

14. Checkout Support

If you experience a problem with a TheBestBundle purchase or checkout, please contact us with relevant transaction information so we can review the issue.
